title=No XP From Life and Living (VHS Still Teaches) [B42]
description=[h1]Life and Living teaches nothing. Tapes still do.[/h1]
description=The [i]Life and Living[/i] TV channel no longer gives skill XP. VHS tapes and CDs keep working exactly like in vanilla.
description=
description=[h2]Why?[/h2]
description=The game has a single sandbox option for all media XP ([i]Maximum Media XP Level[/i]). You cannot switch off the free XP from the first-days [i]Life and Living[/i] shows without turning off VHS tapes as well. This mod splits the two: no more planning your first week around the TV schedule, while finding and playing tapes is still worth it.
description=
description=[h2]What changes[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Life and Living:[/b] skill XP is removed. Boredom, mood and recipes from the shows still apply. The same goes for any other TV or radio broadcast with skill XP, including channels added by mods (in vanilla only Life and Living has any).
description=[*][b]VHS tapes and CDs:[/b] unchanged and still limited by [i]Maximum Media XP Level[/i]. Tapes added by other mods keep their XP too.
description=[*]A line you have already heard still counts as heard, like in vanilla.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Good to know[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*]Singleplayer and multiplayer. On a server, add it to the server's mods; players download it automatically.
description=[*]Safe to add to or remove from an existing save. No items, no sandbox options, nothing to configure.
description=[*]Tested in game: all 157 Life and Living lines with skill XP give none, and all 338 skill lines from the 62 teaching tapes still give XP.
description=[*]Tiny Lua-only mod. It does not replace any game file: it filters the game's own TV and radio text handler, so it should get along with other TV and radio mods.
description=[/list]
